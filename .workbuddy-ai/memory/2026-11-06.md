## Weight read (WSP) — the problem is three exact zeros

Read the accepted checkpoint's numbers directly, then tested causally.

- `t_bias` (5x8) is **40 exact zeros**. Forward pass confirms the consequence:
  `max |target[s] - target[0]| = 0.0` over all spell slots — the target head ranks targets once and
  reuses that ranking for every spell. #142 is not a weak head, it is a zero table.
- `spell/1` bias max abs **1.451e-3**; spell columns are not collapsed (cosines 0.004–0.195).
- `blind = (mechanics, summons, pool)` zeroes 32 of 81 actor inputs and 4 of 13 globals.
  `tools/read_weights.py --init-seed 1` reports `blinded_input_l2_from_init: 0.0`: those encoder rows
  are bit-identical to initialization, because a zero input gives a zero gradient.
- Paired unblind test (same weights, same 18,319 states): spell agreement 39.59% → **36.31%**,
  3,714 choices change, 915 right vs 1,516 wrong. Unmasking injects frozen noise; it is not a fix.
- Head is confidently wrong, not tied: on 8,396 rows wanting spell 0 the net's excess is median
  1.952 logits (73.5% > 1.0, 49% > 2.0); the bias fit needed 6.25–8.00.
- **All 27 party checkpoints on disk are blind.** The only visible arm, `mech_swarm_visible_s1.pkl`,
  records `allies=1` against a 4v4 corpus and cannot be instantiated on it (shape 5 vs 8) — its
  provenance is inconsistent, so it is not a usable counterfactual.

Conclusion: no weight write fixes this (bias infeasible, unmasking hurts, t_bias worth +1.5 pp).
The missing thing is the training run nobody did: party corpus with `--blind ""`.

## Kaggle mechanics that cost time

- Datasets mount at `/kaggle/input/datasets/<owner>/<slug>` on the current image, not the flat
  `/kaggle/input/<slug>`. Resolve by rglob, never by literal path.
- `kaggle datasets create --dir-mode zip` keeps the folder prefix; the *files listing* shows
  `code/...`, but do not assume the mount path either way — check `datasets files`.
- The repo's `combat/__init__.py` imports `_lazy_exports`; ship a trimmed package marker in any
  Kaggle bundle or the import dies before training starts.
- GPU T4×2 handled 1,024 boards × 60 iters in 170 s where the local box OOM'd at 11.9 GB.
